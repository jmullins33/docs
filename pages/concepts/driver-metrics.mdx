# Driver Metrics

All historical driver metrics are derived from **DIRTRACKR**, the primary structured results database for Lucas Oil Late Models and affiliated dirt late model series. DIRTRACKR data is ingested nightly via batch pull.

---

## Core metrics used in the odds engine

### Form score

A rolling 0–100 score representing recent performance over the past 30 days relative to career baseline.

- Weighted by field depth (winning a 30-car national show counts more than a 12-car regional)
- Decayed by recency — last weekend counts more than three weekends ago
- Normalized within the division so scores are comparable across drivers

### Track affinity score

A 0–100 score measuring how well a driver performs at a specific track relative to the rest of the field.

- Weighted for recency — results from the past two seasons count double
- Accounts for field depth at each event
- Drivers with fewer than 3 starts at a venue are regressed toward their overall profile

### Surface performance split

Every driver's record is segmented by condition band — this is the key input to condition-adjusted odds:

| Surface | Score Range | Win Rate | Avg Finish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heavy | 61–100 | — | — |
| Tacky | 41–60 | — | — |
| Slick | 0–40 | — | — |

A driver who dominates tacky conditions but struggles on a slick gets a lower win probability when the model projects a dry, one-groove feature.

## Extended metrics

### Fatigue index

Measures physical workload over the past 7 days — counts starts, travel distance, and race miles. High fatigue (index > 80) mildly penalizes projected performance.

### Starting position impact

- **Front-row conversion rate**: how often a driver converts a P1–P4 start to a win
- **Charge-through rate**: average positions gained from P10+ starts

### Heat race calibration

When heat race results are available:

- Heat race finish vs. expected finish (over/underperformance)
- Observed lap times vs. field median (timing feed required)
- Car damage or mechanical issues flagged in timing data

## Driver database coverage

| Series | Since | Completeness |
|---|---|---|
| Lucas Oil Late Models | 2010 | Full finishing order, DNFs, DNQs |
| World of Outlaws STS | 2012 | Full finishing order |
| CARS Tour | 2015 | Full finishing order |
| Regional / outlaw late models | Varies | Best-effort via DIRTRACKR submissions |

<Note>
  Drivers with fewer than 5 career starts in the division have wider confidence intervals. The model regresses their prior toward the population mean rather than excluding them.
</Note>

GET /drivers/{driver_id}/stats/condition-split?series=lucas_oil_late_models
GET /drivers?search=overton&series=lucas_oil_late_models

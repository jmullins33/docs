---
title: "Predicting winners"
description: "Generate win probabilities for a Lucas Oil LMDS feature"
---

Once each entry has a [rating](/predictions/methodology), predicting the winner is
a matter of converting ratings into probabilities for the specific field that
showed up.

## The steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build the field">
    Pull the entry list for the race. Only the drivers actually entered should be
    scored — a strong driver who skipped the event doesn't count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rate every entry">
    Compute each driver's rating using their recent form, track fit, qualifying
    pace, and team strength for this event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Convert to probabilities">
    Apply the softmax so the win probabilities across the field sum to 100%.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rank and review">
    Sort by probability. Sanity-check that known aces sit near the top and the
    gap between favorites and the field looks reasonable.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example output

| Rank | Driver | Win probability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Jonathan Davenport | 24% |
| 2 | Brandon Sheppard | 19% |
| 3 | Tim McCreadie | 14% |
| 4 | Bobby Pierce | 11% |
| 5 | Devin Moran | 8% |
| ... | field | 24% |

<Note>
  Win probabilities are spread across the whole field, so even a strong favorite
  usually lands well under 50%. That's expected — dirt features are volatile.
</Note>

## Improving accuracy

- **Use qualifying once it's posted.** Adding actual time-trial results before the
  feature sharpens predictions dramatically.
- **Account for starting position.** A fast car buried deep in the field has a
  lower win chance than its rating alone suggests.
- **Watch for no-shows and provisionals.** A late field change shifts every
  probability.

For everything beyond the win, see [predicting props](/predictions/props).

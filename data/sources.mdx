The predictions are only as good as the data behind them. This page covers the
sources to pull from and how to keep them current.

## Primary sources

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Official series site" icon="flag-checkered" href="https://www.lucasdirt.com">
    Schedule, entry lists, points standings, and official results.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Race recaps" icon="newspaper">
    Post-race summaries that confirm feature winners, fast time, and hard charger.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## What to collect per race

For every event, capture enough to fill the [schema](/data/schema):

<Steps>
  <Step title="Event details">
    Date, track, feature lap count, and purse.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Entry list">
    Driver, car number, and team for each entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Qualifying">
    Time-trial seconds and qualifying position.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finishing results">
    Start position, finish position, laps led, and status (running / DNF / DNS / DQ).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keeping data current

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="calendar" title="Sync the schedule first">
    Load the season schedule up front so each result has a race to attach to.
    Re-check it periodically — rainouts and reschedules are common on dirt.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="rotate" title="Ingest results within 24 hours">
    Pull each race in soon after it runs while recaps are fresh. Recent form is
    weighted heavily by the model, so timeliness matters.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="circle-check" title="Validate before scoring">
    Confirm the field size, that exactly one car is marked the winner, and that
    laps led never exceed the feature lap count.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
  Always respect each source's terms of use and rate limits when collecting data.
  Cache responses rather than re-scraping the same race repeatedly.
</Warning>

Once raw data is in, normalize it on the [Drivers & tracks](/data/drivers-tracks) page.

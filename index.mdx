## What is Dirt IQ?

Dirt IQ is a **B2B odds data feed** for dirt track racing. We ingest historical results from DIRTRACKR and live event data to produce opening lines, probability-weighted odds, and real-time line adjustments for sportsbook operators.

Think Amelco or IMG Arena — but purpose-built for the niche motorsports gap that major odds compilers ignore.

## Why dirt track racing

Niche motorsports represent an underserved but growing betting market. Established odds compilers — Amelco, Sportech, IMG Arena — do not cover Lucas Oil Late Models, World of Outlaws, or regional short-track series. Sportsbooks that want to offer these markets either build in-house or go without lines entirely.

Dirt IQ fills that gap with a defensible data moat: structured results history going back to 2010, track-level surface modeling, and real-time integration with timing systems that major data providers don't touch.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ingest historical results">
    DIRTRACKR data is ingested, cleaned, and loaded into the Dirt IQ results database. Driver and track records are normalized across series and seasons.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate opening lines">
    When an entry list is published, the Bayesian odds engine scores each driver against field depth, track affinity, and recent form — producing probability estimates that are converted to opening moneyline and head-to-head odds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust lines in real time">
    As qualifying, heat races, and track conditions come in, the model recalculates. Your trading desk receives updated odds via the API before your book gets exposure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Flag sharp action">
    The sharp money detection algorithm monitors deviation between book position and model probability, surfacing alerts when a market looks mispriced or when unusual volume is concentrating on specific drivers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What is Dirt IQ?

Dirt IQ is a predictive analytics platform built specifically for dirt track racing. Whether you're a team owner making setup decisions, a promoter optimizing race cards, or a fan looking for data-driven picks, Dirt IQ puts the power of machine learning and real-time data at your fingertips.

## How it works

Dirt IQ ingests data from multiple sources — historical race results, live timing feeds, weather stations, and track prep logs — and runs them through a suite of purpose-built ML models. Outputs are available as a REST API, a live dashboard, and webhook events that fire as race conditions change throughout the night.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your data sources">
    Link timing systems, weather stations, and your race management software via our integrations catalog or the REST API.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate predictions">
    Call `/predictions` with an event ID and division to receive ranked win probabilities, confidence scores, and key contributing factors for each driver.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act on real-time updates">
    Subscribe to webhook events and receive updated predictions automatically as track conditions evolve from hot laps through the feature event.
  </Step>
</Steps>

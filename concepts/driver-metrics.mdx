## Overview

Driver performance metrics are the foundation of every prediction. Dirt IQ maintains a continuously updated profile for each driver that goes well beyond raw win counts — capturing surface preferences, form trajectories, track affinities, and more.

All metrics are available via the `/drivers/{id}` endpoint and are embedded in every prediction response.

---

## Core metrics

### Form score

A rolling performance score from **0 to 100** representing how well a driver has been performing over the past 30 days relative to their career baseline.

- Calculated from finish positions weighted by field depth (winning a 30-car regional show counts more than winning a 12-car show)
- Decayed by recency — last weekend counts more than three weekends ago
- Normalized to 100 within the division so scores are comparable across drivers

```
form_score: 91.2  // high — driver is in exceptional recent form
form_score: 54.0  // average — consistent with career baseline
form_score: 22.7  // low — below-average recent run of results
```

### Track affinity score

A score from **0 to 100** measuring how well a driver performs at a specific track relative to the rest of the field. High affinity means the driver consistently outperforms expectations at that venue.

- Tracks driver results at this specific venue over their career
- Weighted for recency — results from the past two seasons count double
- Accounts for field depth at each event

### Surface performance breakdown

Every driver's stats are split by surface type:

| Surface | Win Rate | Avg Finish | Laps Led % |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heavy clay (score 61–100) | — | — | — |
| Tacky clay (score 41–60) | — | — | — |
| Slick clay (score 0–40) | — | — | — |

This breakdown is the key input to the track condition model — a driver who dominates tacky conditions but struggles on a slick gets a lower win probability when the model projects a dry, one-groove feature.

## Extended metrics

### Fatigue index

Measures physical workload over the past 7 days:

- Counts starts, travel distance, and race miles in the window
- High fatigue (index > 80) mildly penalizes projected performance
- Most relevant for drivers on grueling summer travel schedules

### Starting position impact

Some drivers are significantly better from the front; others are exceptional at threading through traffic:

- **Front-row advantage**: how much better a driver performs starting P1–P4 vs. P5+
- **Passing grade**: historical success rate advancing through the field from mid-pack and back

### Heat race calibration

When heat race results are available (and `include_heat_race_data: true` is set), the model factors in:

- Heat race finish vs. expected finish (did the driver over- or under-perform?)
- Observed lap times relative to competitors (timing feed required)
- Car damage or mechanical issues flagged in the timing data

## Accessing driver data

```bash
# Get a driver's full profile
GET /drivers/drv_9

# Search drivers by name
GET /drivers?search=schatz

# Get a driver's history at a specific track
GET /drivers/drv_9/history?track_id=trk_knoxville
```

### Example response

```json
{
  "driver_id": "drv_9",
  "name": "Donny Schatz",
  "hometown": "Fargo, ND",
  "career_starts": 3241,
  "career_wins": 631,
  "career_win_rate": 0.195,
  "form_score": 88.3,
  "surface_stats": {
    "heavy": { "win_rate": 0.21, "avg_finish": 3.1 },
    "tacky": { "win_rate": 0.19, "avg_finish": 3.4 },

  },
  "fatigue_index": 42.0,
  "last_updated": "2024-08-10T22:14:00Z"
}
```

## Driver database coverage

Dirt IQ maintains profiles for:

- **World of Outlaws STS** — full roster, all seasons since 2010
- **USAC National** — full roster, all seasons since 2012
- **iRacing Dirt** — 2018 to present
- **Regional series** — varies by series; contact us to add coverage

Drivers are matched across series by name and hometown — aliases and maiden names are accounted for. If a driver is missing or has a duplicate, report it via the dashboard.

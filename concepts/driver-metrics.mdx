## Data source

All historical driver metrics are derived from **DIRTRACKR**, the primary structured results database for Lucas Oil Late Models and affiliated dirt late model series. DIRTRACKR maintains:

- Full finishing order for every Lucas Oil national event
- Heat race, consolation, and qualifying results
- Car number and team changes over time
- DNF, DNQ, and provisional finish flags

Dirt IQ ingests DIRTRACKR data nightly via batch pull. Live timing data (lap times, real-time position) is ingested during race nights via direct timing system integrations.

## Core metrics used in the odds engine

### Career win rate (series-adjusted)

Raw win rate normalized for field depth. Winning a 35-car Lucas Oil national event receives a higher weight than winning a 15-car regional show.

```
adjusted_win_rate = wins / starts × field_depth_multiplier
```

Field depth multiplier is calculated as `avg_field_size / 20` (20-car field = 1.0 baseline).

### Rolling form score

A 0–100 score representing recent performance relative to career baseline, with exponential decay (half-life = 90 days). The form score is the primary recency signal in the Bayesian prior.

### Track affinity score

Win rate and average finish at a specific track, weighted for recency. Drivers with 10+ starts at a venue have a well-calibrated affinity score; those with fewer than 3 starts are given a prior regressed toward their overall profile.

### Surface performance split

Every driver's record is segmented by condition band:

| Band | Score Range | Win Rate | Avg Finish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heavy | 61–100 | — | — |
| Tacky | 41–60 | — | — |
| Slick | 0–40 | — | — |

This split is the primary input to the condition-adjusted odds. A driver who is strong across all bands is a reliable favorite; a driver with a pronounced split is treated as a conditional favorite.

### Starting position history

Starting position has a measurable effect on win probability in dirt track racing — track position is often decisive on a slick surface. The model maintains:

- **Front-row win conversion rate**: how often a driver converts a P1–P4 start to a win
- **Charge-through rate**: average positions gained from P10+ starts

## Metrics used for line adjustment (post-qualifying)

Once qualifying or time trials are complete, the following additional metrics come into play:

| Metric | Signal |
|---|---|
| Qualifying time delta | Driver's lap time vs. field median — pace signal independent of starting position |
| Heat race result vs. expected | Over/underperformance in heat relative to model prediction |
| Observed lap time in heat | Raw speed signal when live timing is connected |

## Metrics not currently modeled

| Factor | Status |
|---|---|
| Crew chief / team changes | Flagged manually via operator notes; included as binary feature |
| Tire compound selection | Not captured in DIRTRACKR; roadmap item |
| Car ownership / engine builder | Partially modeled via team historical performance |
| Injury / physical condition | Not modeled; treated as data gap |

Missing data is handled by regressing to career averages rather than penalizing drivers with incomplete records.

## Accessing driver profiles

```bash
GET /drivers/{driver_id}
GET /drivers/{driver_id}/history?track_id=trk_eldora
GET /drivers?search=overton&series=lucas_oil_late_models
```

Driver profiles are included in every odds response under `drivers[].driver_profile` when the `expand=driver_profile` query parameter is passed.
